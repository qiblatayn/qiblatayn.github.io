Questions

Does Qiblatayn work without internet?

Yes. Once your location is known, prayer times and the qibla are pure calculation on the phone.

Why do my times differ from another app?

Different calculation methods. Choose the one your mosque uses, or add a minute offset per prayer.

How accurate is the qibla compass?

As accurate as your phone's compass allows. Qiblatayn corrects magnetic north to true north with the World Magnetic Model, shows when the sensor needs calibrating, and guides you through a quick figure-eight. Keep away from metal and magnets while you read it.

Which calculation method should I choose?

The one your local mosque or authority uses — for example Umm al-Qura in Saudi Arabia, Diyanet in Turkey, ISNA in North America. If a time still differs by a minute or two, add an offset to that prayer.

Does Qiblatayn share my location?

No. Your location is used on the phone to calculate times and the qibla, and it is never sent to us or anyone else. You can also type a city by hand instead of using GPS.

Why is the Hijri date one day off?

Months begin with a moon sighting, which differs between countries. Use the Hijri date adjustment in Settings to match your community.

Will a travel pass renew by itself?

No. A 7, 14 or 30-day pass simply ends. Only the monthly and yearly options renew, and they can be cancelled at any time in your store account.

I changed phones. How do I get Premium back?

Install Qiblatayn, open Premium and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same store account you bought it with.

What does the name mean?

Masjid al-Qiblatayn in Madinah is where the direction of prayer turned from Jerusalem to Makkah.
